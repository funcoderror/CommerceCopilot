
# Return and Refund Policy

## 1. Return Eligibility

Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving an eligible product.

Products must be:
- Unused and in their original condition.
- Returned with original packaging.
- Accompanied by proof of purchase.

## 2. Non-Returnable Products

The following products are generally not eligible for returns:
- Personal care and hygiene products once opened.
- Products damaged due to customer misuse.
- Products explicitly marked as non-returnable.

## 3. Return Process

1. Contact customer support with your order ID.
2. Explain the reason for requesting a return.
3. Support will review the request.
4. If approved, return instructions will be provided.
5. The product will be inspected after collection.

## 4. Refund Policy

Refunds are initiated after the returned product passes inspection.

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5-7 business days.

Refunds are issued through the original payment method wherever possible.

## 5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If a customer receives a damaged or incorrect product, they should contact support within 48 hours of delivery.

Customers may be asked to provide photographs as evidence.

Eligible cases will be reviewed for replacement or refund.

## 6. Order Cancellation

Customers can request cancellation before an order is shipped.

Shipped orders generally cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process and may require a return request after delivery.
