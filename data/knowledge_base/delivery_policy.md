
# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within 1-2 business days after confirmation.

Processing time may vary during holidays and high-demand periods.

## 2. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery times:

- Standard delivery: 3-7 business days after dispatch.
- Remote locations may require additional delivery time.

Actual delivery dates depend on the shipping location and courier availability.

## 3. Order Tracking

Customers can track eligible shipped orders using their tracking ID.

Tracking information becomes available after the courier receives the package.

## 4. Order Status

The store uses the following order statuses:

- Pending: Order received but awaiting confirmation or processing.
- Processing: Order is being prepared.
- Shipped: Order has been handed over to the courier.
- Delivered: Order has reached the customer.
- Cancelled: Order has been cancelled.

## 5. Delayed Deliveries

Delivery delays may occur because of:
- Weather conditions.
- Courier service disruptions.
- Incorrect delivery addresses.
- Regional transportation issues.

Customers experiencing delays should contact customer support with their order ID.

## 6. Delivery Address

Customers should ensure that their delivery address and contact information are correct when placing an order.

Address changes may not be possible once an order has been shipped.
