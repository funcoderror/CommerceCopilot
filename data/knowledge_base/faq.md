
# Frequently Asked Questions

## Products

### How can I check product availability?

Product availability depends on the current stock level.

A product with stock greater than zero is available for purchase.

A product with zero stock is currently out of stock.

### How can I find products within my budget?

Customers can search for products using their preferred budget.

For example:
- Show me headphones under Rs. 2000.
- Find smartphones under Rs. 15000.
- Show me discounted electronics.

Product prices and discounts are available in the product catalogue.

### How do I check product specifications?

Customers can refer to the individual product description and specifications.

For compatibility-related questions, the exact device model should be checked against the product specifications.

If compatibility information is unavailable, customers should contact support before purchasing.

## Orders

### How can I check my order status?

Customers can provide their order ID to check the current order status.

Available statuses include Pending, Processing, Shipped, Delivered and Cancelled.

### How can I track my order?

Customers with shipped orders can use their tracking ID to track their package.

Tracking information may take some time to become available after dispatch.

### Can I cancel my order?

Orders can generally be cancelled before shipping.

Once an order has been shipped, customers may need to follow the return process instead.

## Payments

### What payment methods are supported?

The demonstration store supports the following payment methods as policy examples:

- UPI
- Debit cards
- Credit cards
- Net banking
- Cash on delivery, where available

Actual payment processing is not implemented in this prototype.

### What do payment statuses mean?

- Paid: Payment has been successfully recorded.
- Pending: Payment is awaiting confirmation.
- Failed: Payment attempt was unsuccessful.
- Refunded: Payment has been refunded.

### How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds are generally processed within 5-7 business days.

## Warranty

### Do products include a warranty?

Warranty coverage depends on the manufacturer and individual product.

Customers should check the product listing or manufacturer warranty documentation.

### How can I claim a warranty?

Customers should contact support with:
- Order ID.
- Product details.
- Purchase information.
- Description of the issue.

Warranty eligibility will be reviewed according to the manufacturer's terms.

## General Support

### How can I contact customer support?

Customers should provide their order ID or product ID when contacting support.

This helps the support team identify the relevant transaction or product.

### What if the chatbot cannot answer my question?

If the requested information is unavailable in the product catalogue, order records or knowledge base, the chatbot should acknowledge the limitation instead of inventing an answer.
